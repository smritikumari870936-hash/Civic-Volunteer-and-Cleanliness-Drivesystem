Why We Started This Initiative

A clean environment is everyone's responsibility. Our initiative aims to bring people together to protect nature, improve public spaces and create cleaner surroundings through community participation. By organizing lake cleanups, park beautification, wall restoration and tree plantation drives, we encourage citizens to take small but meaningful steps towards a healthier and greener future.
